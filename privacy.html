Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 • PlayZone is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe in an open web that respects user confidentiality.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PlayZone (accessible at https://playzone-mauve-ten.vercel.app/). We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a secure, safe, and transparent gaming environment for all visitors.

This Privacy Policy outlines how PlayZone handles information, what technologies we utilize, and your rights when enjoying our browser-based mini-games.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

PlayZone is fundamentally engineered as a client-side, privacy-first web application:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create a profile, or provide an email address, username, or password to play our games.
  • No Payment Details: All games on PlayZone are 100% free. We never request credit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial information.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: We do not deploy behavioral tracking cookies, commercial advertising beacons, or third-party pixel trackers.

3. How We Use LocalStorage (On-Device Storage)

To make your gaming experience seamless and save your progress across sessions, PlayZone utilizes your web browser's native HTML5 LocalStorage API. This information is stored exclusively on your local device and is never sent to or stored on any external server.

The exact LocalStorage keys used by PlayZone and their purposes are:

  • playzone_score_snake — Stores the single highest score (integer number) achieved in Neon Snake.
  • playzone_score_memory — Stores the overall best completion time (integer seconds) across games in Memory Flip, displayed on the homepage card.
  • playzone_score_memory_easy — Stores the best completion time (seconds) specifically for Easy mode (8 cards) in Memory Flip.
  • playzone_score_memory_medium — Stores the best completion time (seconds) specifically for Medium mode (12 cards) in Memory Flip.
  • playzone_score_memory_hard — Stores the best completion time (seconds) specifically for Hard mode (16 cards) in Memory Flip.
  • playzone_score_tictactoe — Stores the total number of human player wins against the AI (integer) in Tic-Tac-Toe, displayed on the homepage card.
  • playzone_ttt_stats — Stores a JSON-formatted string of match statistics for Tic-Tac-Toe: {"xWins": 0, "oWins": 0, "draws": 0, "playerWins": 0}.
  • playzone_score_reaction — Stores the fastest single reaction time (integer milliseconds) recorded in Reflex Speed Test.
  • playzone_sound — Stores your audio preference: "enabled" or "disabled".
Managing Your Saved Data

Because this data exists purely within your local browser, you can reset or delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data/cache, or using the "Clear Record" / "Reset Scores" buttons provided directly within each game.

4. Hosting & Server Logs

PlayZone is hosted as a static web application on Vercel. Like standard web hosting services, Vercel's edge network automatically processes incoming web requests to serve website files and may record standard technical server access logs (such as IP addresses, user-agent strings, requested URLs, and request timestamps) for network security, troubleshooting, and infrastructure monitoring.

5. Third-Party Services & Assets

PlayZone keeps external service requests to a minimum:

  • Google Fonts: Typography (Outfit and Space Grotesk) is loaded from Google's font servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser requests these font files, your IP address and browser headers are transmitted to Google in accordance with Google's standard privacy policies.
  • Procedural Audio: Sound effects are synthesized entirely in code inside your browser via the native Web Audio API. Zero external audio hosting servers are queried.
  • Analytics & Ads: PlayZone does NOT run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising networks, or any commercial user tracking scripts.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayZone provides casual, family-friendly mini-games intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under 13 years of age, or from users of any age.

Because PlayZone operates purely in your browser without user accounts, profiles, registration forms, or backend databases, no personal identifiers are gathered. If you have any questions regarding privacy on PlayZone, please contact us at jethwaisha60@gmail.com.
