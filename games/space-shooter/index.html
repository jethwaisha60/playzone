About Space Shooter

Space Shooter is an adrenaline-fueled vertical arcade shmup (shoot 'em up) rendered in vivid retro-neon styling. Commanding an advanced starfighter across deep cosmic voids, players intercept relentless waves of hostile cyber-invaders, weaving through dense laser barrages and claiming high-score glory.

How to Play

Maneuver your ship along the lower perimeter of the battlefield while unleashing rapid laser volleys at incoming enemy squadrons. Defeat aliens before they breach defensive lines or collide with your hull. Collect falling power-up capsules to enhance firepower, restore shields, and survive escalating attack waves.

Game Controls

Desktop (Keyboard)

  • Arrow Left / Right or A / D: Steer starfighter laterally
  • Spacebar: Fire primary photon cannons
  • P / Escape: Pause or resume the cosmic combat

Mobile & Touch

  • Direction Buttons: Tap Left (◀) and Right (▶) virtual pedals
  • FIRE Button: Tap or hold the dedicated weapon trigger
  • Direct Drag: Touch and slide your finger across the canvas to steer

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Focus Fire on Dive-Bombers: Target aggressive dive-bombing drones first, as their erratic trajectories represent the highest collision risk.
  • Stay Centered: Keep your ship near the center-bottom of the screen; corner trapping leaves zero escape vectors when enemy bombs rain down.
  • Collect Falling Upgrades: Weapon upgrade capsules significantly increase projectile spread and rate of fire, essential for handling high-density swarms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do power-ups improve weapon systems?

Yes. Collecting power-up modules upgrades your standard single blaster into dual cannons and spread lasers while replenishing vital shield integrity.

Does the difficulty scale indefinitely?

Enemy flight velocity, firing frequency, and swarm counts increase progressively with every wave cleared, demanding razor-sharp reflexes.

Is my top score saved automatically?

Yes. Your highest arcade kill score is automatically saved to local storage so you can compete against your personal best at any time.
