About Neon Whack-a-Mole

Neon Whack-a-Mole is a rapid-fire reflex arcade challenge reimagined in vibrant neon aesthetics. Set across a 3×3 grid of subterranean cyber-burrows, players must strike emerging mechanical critters with pinpoint precision while exercising impulse control to avoid hazardous explosive decoys.

How to Play

Click or tap moles the instant they pop out of the cyber-holes before they submerge back into hiding. Every round lasts for a heart-pounding 30 seconds. Standard cyber moles award 10 points, rare luminous golden moles grant a massive 50-point bonus, and accidental strikes on hazard bombs deduct 20 points from your score.

Game Controls

Desktop (Mouse)

  • Left Click: Whack emerging targets with a cyber-mallet strike
  • Spacebar: Rapid pause or resume during gameplay
  • Restart Button: Reset the 30-second clock and scores instantly

Mobile & Touch

  • Finger Tap: Tap directly onto targets across the 9 burrows
  • Multi-Touch Support: Use both thumbs simultaneously for maximum speed
  • Haptic Feedback: Satisfying sensory pops on successful whacks

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Hover in the Center: Resting your mouse cursor near hole #5 (the center hole) minimizes cursor travel distance to all surrounding eight burrows.
  • Golden Mole Priority: Golden moles vanish faster than standard moles; prioritize them immediately for the lucrative 50-point windfall.
  • Trigger Discipline: In the final 10 seconds, spawn rates accelerate dramatically; resist knee-jerk clicking to avoid costly bomb deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the different targets score?

Standard Cyber Moles give +10 points, Golden Moles give +50 points, and Hazard Bombs subtract -20 points from your total.

How long does a standard match last?

Each session runs for exactly 30 seconds, providing a bite-sized, competitive reflex test ideal for quick play.

Is my highest score recorded?

Yes. Your all-time personal best is stored locally in your browser so you can challenge your friends or top your own leaderboard score.
