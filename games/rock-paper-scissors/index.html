About Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors transforms the universal schoolyard dispute-settler into a stylish cyber-arcade duel. Boasting dramatic shake-and-reveal animations, pulsing neon icons, and an analytical AI engine, players challenge computational psychology across competitive best-of series.

How to Play

Select your move—Rock (✊), Paper (✋), or Scissors (✌️)—using the cyber-buttons. The computer reveals its play simultaneously. The standard universal hierarchy applies: Rock crushes Scissors, Scissors cuts Paper, and Paper wraps Rock. Matching moves result in a draw. Compete across 3, 5, or 10-round series to claim the match championship bonus.

Game Controls

Desktop (Mouse & Keyboard)

  • Selection Buttons: Click Rock (✊), Paper (✋), or Scissors (✌️)
  • Match Series Bar: Pick 3-round, 5-round, or 10-round duration
  • AI Level: Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard AI intelligence

Mobile & Touch

  • Action Pad: Large, comfortable touch cards for hand gestures
  • Animated Reveal: Real-time showdown choreography on mobile viewports
  • Score Tracking: Live tally updates for wins, draws, and losses

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Defeat the Hard AI: Hard AI models human psychology: players frequently shift to the option that would have beaten their previous move; break patterns with intentional randomness.
  • Avoid Habitual Repeats: Avoid picking the same gesture three times in a row, as higher-tier AI algorithms detect and punish predictable repetition.
  • Capitalize on Series Length: In 10-round marathons, use the opening two rounds to scout the AI's counter-biases before committing to a dominant strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI differ between difficulty settings?

Easy picks moves with pure 33% randomness, Medium balances random play with occasional tactical responses, and Hard analyzes your move history to predict your next choice.

How does the points scoring system work?

Each round victory awards 10 points, draws yield 2 points, and winning an entire multi-round match secures a massive +50 point champion bonus.

Are all-time statistics saved?

Yes. Your overall match victories, total points record, and win streak are stored locally in your browser storage.
