About Cyber Minesweeper

Cyber Minesweeper is a high-tech modern realization of the quintessential logic-deduction game. Set upon an electric 9×9 cyber-grid hiding 10 armed mines, players use numerical telemetry clues to safely disarm the perimeter, isolate threats, and clear the safe sector in record time.

How to Play

Click or tap an unrevealed tile to expose the ground underneath. If safe, the cell displays a color-coded digit showing exactly how many mines inhabit its surrounding eight neighboring squares. Empty tiles trigger an automatic cascade revealing adjacent safe sectors. Mark suspected mines with flags and uncover every non-mine cell to win.

Game Controls

Desktop (Mouse)

  • Left Click: Reveal an unexposed square
  • Right Click: Plant or retract a hazard warning flag (🚩)
  • Chording (Click Number): Quick-reveal all unflagged neighbors once flags match

Mobile & Touch

  • Tap: Reveal target grid tile
  • Flag Mode Toggle: Tap the Flag button to switch between uncovering and flagging
  • Long Press: Press and hold any cell for 400ms to drop a flag directly

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Guaranteed Safe Openings: The engine generates the minefield after your initial click, guaranteeing you will never detonate on turn one.
  • Master the 1-2 Pattern: When numbers 1 and 2 appear side by side along an unexplored wall, deductive geometry frequently isolates the mine position without guessing.
  • Use Chording for Speed: Once all adjacent mines for a number are flagged, click the number itself to uncover all remaining neighbors instantaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I detonate a mine on my very first click?

No. Cyber Minesweeper employs smart first-click protection: mines are seeded only after your opening tile is picked, ensuring a safe starting opening every round.

How does the interactive face button work?

The status face at the top mirrors game state (surprised on click, joyful on victory, downed on loss) and doubles as an instant restart button.

Does the timer record personal best times?

Yes. The digital clock begins tracking seconds from your first click, and your fastest victory completion time is automatically recorded locally.
