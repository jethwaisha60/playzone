About Word Guess

Word Guess is an elegant Wordle-style five-letter vocabulary deduction puzzle crafted with glowing neon aesthetics. Tasked with uncovering a concealed five-letter English word within six guesses, players combine linguistic intuition, letter-frequency analysis, and process-of-elimination logic.

How to Play

Enter any valid five-letter English word and press Enter to submit your guess. With each submission, the tiles flip to reveal essential color-coded telemetry: Green signifies a correct letter in the exact spot; Yellow reveals that the letter belongs in the word but in a different position; and Dark Gray indicates the letter does not appear in the secret word at all. Repeated letters follow strict official counting rules.

Game Controls

Desktop (Keyboard)

  • A–Z Keys: Type letter characters into current row tiles
  • Enter Key: Submit completed 5-letter word for verification
  • Backspace: Delete the most recently entered letter

Mobile & Touch

  • Virtual Keyboard: Tap on-screen letter keys directly
  • Color-Coded Status: Virtual keys tint Green/Yellow/Gray matching discovered clues
  • Enter / Backspace: Dedicated touch buttons for fast submission

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Choose High-Yield Starters: Open with words rich in common vowels and top consonants, such as CRANE, SLATE, or AUDIO, to immediately map out vowel patterns.
  • Avoid Repeating Dark Letters: Never reuse letters flagged gray on earlier lines; test unrevealed letters to narrow down possibilities quickly.
  • Account for Repeated Letters: If a guess has two 'E's and only one glows yellow or green, the mystery word contains exactly one 'E'.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play unlimited games every day?

Yes. Unlike daily-locked word games, PlayZone Word Guess allows unlimited replayability—simply click New Game to generate a fresh mystery word anytime.

How are repeated letters handled in clues?

If you guess a letter multiple times, tiles illuminate yellow or green only up to the exact count that letter appears in the hidden word, preventing false duplicate assumptions.

Are win streaks and statistics saved?

Yes. The game saves games played, win rate percentage, current win streak, and maximum streak directly to your local browser storage.
