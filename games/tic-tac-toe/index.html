About Tic-Tac-Toe AI

Tic-Tac-Toe AI delivers a polished cyberpunk interpretation of the timeless pencil-and-paper strategy game. Featuring glowing neon cyan crosses and vibrant magenta noughts, players can test their tactical wits against an adaptive artificial intelligence powered by the Minimax algorithm or battle friends locally in head-to-head multiplayer.

How to Play

Players take turns placing their designated symbol (✕ or ○) inside an empty square on the 3×3 grid. The first player to align three matching symbols along any horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal line wins the round. If all nine squares fill without a winning combination, the match concludes in a tactical draw.

Game Controls

Desktop (Mouse)

  • Left Click: Select and mark any open grid cell
  • Mode & Difficulty: Click toggles to select AI Level or 2-Player mode
  • Symbol Select: Choose to play first (✕) or second (○)

Mobile & Touch

  • Tap Cell: Tap any open slot to drop your token
  • Responsive Toggles: Switch game settings easily with large touch buttons
  • Quick Restart: Clear the board or reset scores with one touch

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Seize the Center: Taking the center cell on your first turn opens four potential winning lines and restricts your opponent's options.
  • Construct Forks: Aim to create two simultaneous winning threats (a fork); your opponent can only block one, securing your win on the next turn.
  • Defend the Corners: When going second, take a corner cell if your opponent occupies the center to prevent lethal diagonal traps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the Hard AI be defeated?

Hard mode uses the Minimax algorithm, which evaluates all possible outcomes. If you play perfectly, you will always earn a draw; any human misstep allows the AI to claim victory.

How does the 2 Players mode work?

Switching to 2 Players enables local pass-and-play on the same device, alternating turns between Player ✕ and Player ○ without computer intervention.

Are win/loss statistics saved?

The scoreboard tracks your current session tally of Player X wins, Player O wins, and Draws, with an easy reset option to start fresh anytime.
