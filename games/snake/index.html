About Neon Snake

Neon Snake is a modern cyberpunk reimagining of the legendary retro arcade classic. Rendered in a glowing neon aesthetic, you pilot an agile cybernetic serpent across a high-contrast 20×20 grid. Your mission is to consume pulsating energy orbs, grow your tail, and survive as the game's tempo steadily increases with every bite.

How to Play

Navigate the snake toward glowing food orbs scattered across the grid. Each orb you collect awards 10 points and increases your body length by one segment. Avoid colliding with the grid boundary walls or crashing into your own tail. The game tests your spatial awareness and reaction speed as the movement speed accelerates progressively.

Game Controls

Desktop (Keyboard)

  • Arrow Keys / W, A, S, D: Turn Up, Down, Left, Right
  • Spacebar: Pause / Resume the game
  • Restart Button: Instantly reset the current run

Mobile & Touch

  • Virtual D-Pad: Tap ▲, ▼, ◀, ▶ buttons below the board
  • Touch Swiping: Swipe in any direction across the screen
  • Pause Button: Tap to freeze or resume gameplay

Gameplay Tips & Strategy

  • Circle the Perimeter: As your tail grows longer, patrol outer grid edges to leave open escape paths in the center.
  • Never Double-Back: Turning directly in the opposite direction results in an instant collision with your own body.
  • Use Input Buffering: The engine buffers up to two directional inputs, allowing you to execute tight turns before reaching intersections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game speed up as my score increases?

Yes. Every few food items collected gradually reduces the tick delay from 120ms down to a swift 60ms maximum speed, demanding faster reflexes.

Is my high score automatically saved?

Yes. Your highest record is saved directly to your browser's local storage under the PlayZone key, persisting across visits and restarts.

Can I play Neon Snake offline on mobile?

Once loaded, Neon Snake runs 100% client-side in your mobile browser without streaming external assets, making it smooth and bandwidth-friendly.
